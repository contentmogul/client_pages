Ad spend, revenue, ROAS and contribution

Perk Singapore and Perk Malaysia: the last 6 months of actuals and the next 6 months of the forward plan saved in the Perk Reports tool. Every point is a 28-day window (always exactly 4 Sunday and 4 Tuesday billing days), and the forecast is shown per 28 days too. Each column has three hover areas: the revenue chart at the top, the NCAC and ROAS charts in the middle, and contribution margin and ad spend at the bottom. Each shows only its own figures and how they are worked out.

The three revenue types are stacked: recurring subscriber revenue at the bottom, new subscriber revenue on top of it and one-off revenue on top, so the top edge is total revenue (also given in the popup). The dashed line inside the one-off layer on Chart 2 marks how much of it was ad-attributed.

How each line is measured

Colours: pink = recurring subscribers, purple = new subscribers, red = one-off customers, orange = contribution margin, teal = total blended ROAS, gold = all-customer NCAC, green = one-off ROAS. Filled areas and solid lines = revenue or contribution, dashed = cost per customer or ROAS. No chart colour is black or white, so every line reads the same in light and dark mode.

Ad spend is stacked by what it buys: Meta = subscriber; Google campaigns whose name contains "Subscription" (including the misspelt "Subsicription") = subscriber; every other Google campaign = one-off. Campaigns are classified by their latest name, matching the campaign view. 2024-2025 months only have a Google total, so those bars show it unsplit.

Total blended ROAS = all revenue ÷ all ad spend. One-off ROAS (Google Ads) = ad-attributed one-off revenue (ClickMagick one-off sales sourced from Google, plus the small amount from Meta) ÷ Google one-off campaign spend. ClickMagick only records one-off sales from 25 Jun (MY) / 26 Jun (SG), so the 15 Jun window uses Google one-off spend from those days only. Ratios are blank where the spend was under 2,000 or ads ran fewer than 21 of the 28 days (18 May - 14 Jun: ads started 3 Jun).

New subscribers = subscriptions placing order #1 (every offer). One-off customers = distinct one-off buyers. Subscriber NCAC = subscriber spend ÷ new subscribers. All-customer NCAC = all ad spend ÷ (new subscribers + first-time one-off customers): repeat one-off buyers aren't new customers, so they're left out of the cost per new customer.

Diminishing returns in the forecast: each month's cohort is bought at NCAC = plan NCAC × (that month's spend ÷ today's 28-day spend)0.3 (never below the plan NCAC), and ad-attributed one-off revenue = 7 Sep ROAS × Google one-off spend × (spend ÷ 7 Sep spend)-0.3. So doubling spend raises NCAC 23% and 5× raises it 62%. The 0.3 comes from Perk's own 2024-25 months (new subscribers vs ad spend, organic floor removed: SG 0.41 ± 0.18, MY 0.28 ± 0.13) and matches published cases (an 8× daily-spend increase raised CPA 80%, Saxifrage). It applies to Virgo Small too, since its spend grows 10% a month (2.9× by month 12).

Contribution margin = revenue - product cost - shipping cost where shipping is free, exactly as Perk Reports' own data extract costs each order (perk_sku_pricing, by product and date). It is before ad spend and fixed costs, the same "contribution" the Perk Reports forecaster plots.

New subscriber revenue = orders 1-4 of each subscription (Perk's per-subscription SON- order number); recurring = order 5 onward. The forecast follows the same rule: the plan's new subscribers move into recurring revenue from their 5th order, timed by the measured revenue curve of the 1,030 SG and 2,792 MY subscribers who signed up Jan - Sep 2025 (orders 1-4 vs 5 onward, by days since the first order). Most reach order 5 two to four months after signing up, so recurring revenue starts rising from about the third forecast month.

Sources & caveats

  • Subscription revenue: ecommerce_data.vw_perk_report_orders (shipped + paid). One-off revenue and its contribution: vw_perk_oneoff_splice, which matches WooCommerce's own Jan 1 - Oct 7 2026 sales report within 0.8% (SG) and 0.03% (MY). Ad-attributed one-off revenue: clickmagick_sales_enriched, sale type purchase, source Google or Meta.
  • Excluded as outliers (8 Oct 2026): two Perk Singapore one-off orders for Jura coffee machines, WooCommerce orders 95201 (2 Jun 2026) and 95224 (18 Jun 2026), SGD 2,620 each, one from a corporate customer and one from a former subscriber. They are removed from one-off revenue, contribution, total revenue, blended ROAS and the customer counts everywhere in this report, which lowers SG one-off revenue in the 18 May and 15 Jun windows by SGD 2,620 each. Neither order was ad-attributed, and no forecast assumption used those windows.
  • Ad spend: google_ads_daily + meta_insights_daily (daily, from 3 Jun 2026). Google Shopping campaigns are stored twice per day in google_ads_daily (an ad-level and a campaign-level row with the same spend); each campaign-day is counted once here, which removes MYR 630 and SGD 277 that earlier versions double-counted. 2024-2025 monthly Meta and Google spend from the tracking sheet via perk_analytics.perk_spend_monthly_unified. No ad spend Dec 2025 - 2 Jun 2026.
  • 2024 to Nov 2025 are calendar months (no daily spend exists that far back); a calendar month holds 8 to 10 billing days, so month-to-month swings there partly reflect the calendar.
  • Forecast subscription revenue and contribution come from the Perk Reports tool's own engine (fRun) on the saved plans named above, scaled from its ~30.4-day months to 28 days. Forecast one-off revenue does not use the tool's one-off line, which only grows with new-subscriber volume and has no organic base; the Virgo plans fit their one-off rate to match this report over 12 months.
  • Fixed costs, CM fee and WHT are not shown here; the tool's cash-position view carries those.
